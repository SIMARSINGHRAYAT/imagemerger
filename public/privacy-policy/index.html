Image Merger · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Image Merger is built to process the images you select on your device. This policy explains what happens to those files, what limited information may be handled when you visit this website, and the choices available to you.

Images and files you use

Image files are opened and edited in your browser. The app creates previews and exports using browser memory; it does not upload your images to an Image Merger server or keep a server-side copy. Undo history and editing state are temporary and are held in the current page session.

When you export, the resulting image or PDF is created in your browser and saved to a location you choose using your browser's download or save dialog. Files you download remain subject to your device and browser settings.

Information handled when you visit

The app itself does not ask for an account, collect your name, or send your image contents to us. Like other websites, the hosting provider must handle basic connection information to deliver the site. This may include your IP address, browser and device details, requested page, and request time in server logs, according to the hosting provider's practices and privacy terms.

Fonts and third-party services

The app requests the Inter font from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's font service to retrieve it, which can disclose technical connection information such as your IP address to Google. Google's handling of that request is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. The image files you select are not included in that font request.

Cookies, analytics, and local storage

Image Merger does not set app-specific cookies, use analytics or advertising trackers, or save your images or editing history in local storage. Your browser or the hosting provider may use its own technical storage or logs to operate and secure the website; those are governed by their respective policies.

Retention and your choices

Image data and previews exist in page memory while you use the app and are released when the page is closed or refreshed. You choose which files to open and where to save exports. You can block Google Fonts with browser privacy controls; the app will use a system font instead.

Children's privacy

Image Merger is a general-purpose tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published. For questions about this policy, contact the project maintainer through the Image Merger project on GitHub .
